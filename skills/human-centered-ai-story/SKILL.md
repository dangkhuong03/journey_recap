# Human-Centered AI Story Skill

## Core principle
The intern directs the AI according to their own problem-solving approach.
AI does not replace the intern's reasoning or ownership.

## Correct framing
MY INTENT
→ MY APPROACH / HYPOTHESIS
→ AI SUPPORT
→ HUMAN AUDIT
→ ADJUSTMENT
→ MY DECISION

AI may support:
- research
- idea expansion
- alternative perspectives
- critique
- structure
- consistency checking

Human remains responsible for:
- defining the problem
- deciding scope
- choosing the approach
- evaluating relevance
- rejecting wrong directions
- aligning to intended outcomes
- final judgment

## Avoid
- "AI solved the challenge for me"
- "I avoided AI"
- pretending AI outputs are inherently correct
- implying independent thought means no AI assistance

## Desired message
The learning is not anti-AI. It is about learning to use AI deliberately while keeping human judgment, accountability, and evaluation central.

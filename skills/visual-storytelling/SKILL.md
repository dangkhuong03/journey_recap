# Visual Storytelling Skill

## Goal
Make the viewer understand a transformation, not merely a timeline.

## Method
1. Define the protagonist's starting state.
2. Define the central tension/gap.
3. Select only events that change understanding, behavior, or direction.
4. Identify turning points where a belief, workflow, choice, or outcome changed.
5. Tie each growth claim to evidence.
6. Build toward a visible before/after transformation.
7. End with a grounded present/future direction.

## Required story test
For every major section, answer at least one:
- What happened?
- Why did it matter?
- What changed afterward?
- What evidence supports that change?

If a section answers none, cut or demote it.

## Day 27-specific guardrails
- The protagonist is the intern, not AI20K, not the mentors, not the challenge list.
- Challenges are evidence nodes, not the main character.
- Do not create a fake hero narrative where every event was a success.
- Preserve uncertainty and areas still being learned.
- The engineer mindset remains part of the final identity.

## Output expectation
Produce a section sequence that can be understood by scanning headlines and visuals before reading body copy.
